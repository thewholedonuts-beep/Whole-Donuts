# +U next-step template

## What brought me here?

Write one sentence about what you want support with, learn, make, or share.

## One small next step

Choose an action that takes less than fifteen minutes.

## What would make it easier?

Name one person, tool, place, or piece of information that could help.

## What I can leave for the next person

Share one useful note, connection, or encouragement when you are ready.

This template is free to use, adapt, and share. Do not include private, financial, health, or identifying information unless you choose a service designed to protect it.
